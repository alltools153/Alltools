# 🌸 বাংলা ক্যালেন্ডার ও খাঁটি সনাতন পঞ্জিকা (Bengali Calendar & Interactive Panjika)
> **১৪৩৩ বঙ্গাব্দ (Bongabdo 1433) • সূর্যসিদ্ধান্ত (Gupta Press) ও বিশুদ্ধ সিদ্ধান্ত (Drik) সম্বলিত সর্বাঙ্গীন দিনপঞ্জি**

An authentic, culturally rich, aesthetic, and algorithmically precise **Bengali Calendar & Interactive Panjika (বাংলা ক্যালেন্ডার ও পঞ্জিকা)** web application built with **Next.js 14+ (App Router)**, **TypeScript**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **Web Audio API**.

---

## 🎨 1. Visual Design & Cultural Aesthetics (Pure Bengali Royal Heritage)
- **Palette**:
  - *Parchment / Kagoz* (`#FDFBF7`, `#F8F4EB`, `#F4EBD9`): Evoking century-old handwritten puthi manuscripts.
  - *Sindoor Red / Alta* (`#8B0000`, `#9E1B1B`, `#B91C1C`): Symbolic of auspicious ceremonies, Maa Durga's vermilion, and festive vigor.
  - *Genda Phul / Marigold Amber* (`#F59E0B`, `#EAB308`): Radiant festive marigold garland tones.
  - *Bishnupur Terracotta Clay* (`#8C4830`, `#A75D43`): Earthy terracotta temple brick tones.
  - *Zari Gold / Pitambari* (`#D4AF37`, `#B48A25`): Lustrous gold borders and royal motifs.
- **Handcrafted SVG Motifs**:
  - **Alpona Mandalas & Borders (আলপনা)**: Authentic Bengali floor-painting curves and floral flourishes.
  - **Mangal Ghat (মঙ্গলঘট)**: Auspicious earthen pot with mango leaves, green coconut, and swastika vermilion mark.
  - **Sacred Conch Shell (শঙ্খ)** & **Diya / Pradip Flame (প্রদীপ)** with natural flicker animation.
- **Typography & Font Support**:
  - Bengali typography supporting *Noto Serif Bengali*, *Hind Siliguri*, *Galada*, *Atma*, and *Tiro Bangla*.
  - Dual numerals toggle: Instant switching between Bengali numerals (`১, ২, ৩...`) and English numerals (`1, 2, 3...`).
  - Bilingual UI toggle: Full Bengali (`বাংলা`) and English translation.

## 🌌 2. Panjika Engine & Astronomical Computations (পঞ্জিকা গণনা)
- **Calculation Methods**:
  - **Surya Siddhanta (সূর্যসিদ্ধান্ত)**: Traditional solar-transit basis of Gupta Press & PM Bagchi panjikas.
  - **Drik Siddhanta (দৃক সিদ্ধান্ত)**: Modern observational astronomical calculations.
- **Gregorian to Bongabdo (বঙ্গাব্দ) Converter**:
  - Accurate solar ingress calculations for Poila Boishakh and month day lengths.
  - 12 Bengali Months (*Boishakh, Jyoishtho, Asharh, Srabon, Bhadro, Ashwin, Kartik, Agrahayan, Poush, Magh, Falgun, Choitro*).
  - 6 Bengali Seasons (*Grishma, Barsha, Sharat, Hemanta, Sheet, Basanta*).
  - Era synchronizations: *Bongabdo (১৪৩৩)*, *Sakabdo (১৯৪৮)*, *Tripurabdo (১৪৩৬)*, *Kaliyugabdo (৫১২৭)*.
- **5 Core Panchanga Limbs**:
  - **Tithi (তিথি)**: 30 Tithis across Shukla & Krishna Paksha with start-end times and progress percentage.
  - **Nakshatra (নক্ষত্র)**: 27 Lunar Mansions with ruling lords, Gana (*Deva, Manushya, Rakshasa*), Yoni, Nadi, and symbols.
  - **Yoga (যোগ)**: 27 Yogas with auspicious/inauspicious indicators.
  - **Karana (করণ)**: 11 Karanas (*7 Chara + 4 Sthira*).
  - **Sun & Moon Signs (রবি ও চন্দ্র রাশি)** with exact degrees.
- **Muhurats & Inauspicious Windows**:
  - **Amrita Yoga (অমৃতযোগ)** & **Mahendra Yoga (মাহেন্দ্রযোগ)**
  - **Abhijit Muhurat (অভিজিৎ)**, **Brahma Muhurat (ব্রহ্ম মুহূর্ত)**, **Godhuli (গোধূলি)**
  - **Rahu Kalam (রাহুকাল)**, **Yama Ghantam (যমঘণ্ট)**, **Gulika (গুলিকাকাল)**, **Bara Bela (বারবেলা)**, **Kala Bela (কালবেলা)**
  - **Disha Shool (দিকের শূল)** with traditional Vedic travel remedies.

## 🌺 3. Festive Hub & Interactive Modules
1. **Live Real-time Panjika Widget (আজকের পঞ্জিকা)**: Live ticking clock with Bengali periods (*প্রাতঃ, দুপুর, সন্ধ্যা, নিশীথ*), active Muhurat status beacon, and one-click WhatsApp share.
2. **Interactive Month Grid & Date Picker**: Rich day cells with prominent Bengali numerals, English date tags, tithi badges, and fasting dots.
3. **Durga Puja 2026 Dedicated Hub**:
   - Live countdown timer to *Mahalaya, Maha Shashthi, Maha Ashtami*.
   - Interactive **108 Lotuses & Lamps Sandhi Puja tracker** with exact 48-minute time window.
   - Day-by-day ritual guide from Bodhan to Sindoor Khela & Vijaya Dashami.
4. **Subho Muhurat & Marriage Lagna Finder (শুভ বিবাহ লগ্ন)**: Nightlong lagna timings (*Godhuli, Vrishabha, Mithuna, Simha*), Gotra considerations, Griha Pravesh, Annaprashan, Upanayan, and Sadhbhakhon dates.
5. **Marriage Matchmaking (যোটক বিচার / রাজযোটক ও অষ্টকূট মিলন)**: 36-point Guna score (*Varna, Vashya, Tara, Yoni, Graha Maitri, Gana, Bhakoot, Nadi*), Manglik Dosha analysis, and Vedic remedies.
6. **Bengali Baby Name Finder**: 200+ authentic names filtered by Rashi, Nakshatra, gender, and starting syllables (*পদাক্ষর*) with bookmarks.
7. **Daily Horoscope / Rashiphal (আজকের রাশিফল)**: Comprehensive insights for all 12 signs covering Career, Finance, Love, Health, and Lucky Colors/Numbers.
8. **Date Converter (দ্বিমুখী রূপান্তর)**: Dual-way instant converter between English and Bengali calendar.
9. **Zero-Dependency Web Audio Synthesizer**: Pure Web Audio API synthesized **Shankhadhwani (শঙ্খধ্বনি)**, **Temple Bell (মন্দিরের ঘণ্টা)**, and **Festive Dhak (ঢাকের কাঠি)**.
10. **Printable Bengali Wall Calendar**: Clean, high-resolution printable layout optimized for `@media print` and A4/Letter size export.

## 🚀 5. Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Node.js 18.17+ or 20+
- npm, yarn, or pnpm

### Installation & Run
```bash
# Navigate to the project directory
cd bengali-calendar-panjika

# Install dependencies
npm install

# Run the development server
npm run dev
```

Open [http://localhost:3005](http://localhost:3005) in your browser.
