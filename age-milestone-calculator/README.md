# ⌛ ChronoSphere — Ultimate Age & Milestone Calculator

A Next.js 14, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS web application for calculating exact chronological age, real-time millisecond countdowns, life milestones, cosmic travel statistics, planetary ages across the solar system, astrology lore, 90-year life-in-weeks grid (Memento Mori), and two-person age comparisons.

---

## ✨ Features

### 1. ⏱️ Exact Chronological Age & Live Ticking
- Real-time live updating clock down to the millisecond.
- Detailed breakdown: **Years, Months, Days, Hours, Minutes, Seconds, and Milliseconds**.
- Single-unit metric totals: Total Days, Total Weeks, Total Hours, Total Minutes, and Total Seconds lived.
- Decimal age representation (e.g., `28.4328 years`).
- Day of the week of birth and Leap Year detection.
- Custom target calculation date (calculate age on any historical or future date).

### 2. 🎂 Next Birthday & Half-Birthday Countdown
- Live countdown (Days, Hours, Minutes, Seconds) to turning the next age.
- 6-month **Half-Birthday** tracking.
- Next 7 upcoming birthdays schedule with calendar years, days of the week, and turning ages.

### 3. 🏆 Life & Time Milestones Tracker
- **Age Milestones**: 1st Birthday, 10 Years (Decade), Sweet 16, 18 Years (Adulthood), 21 Years, Quarter Century (25), 30, 40, 50 (Half Century), 60, 65 (Retirement), 75, and 100 Years (Centenarian).
- **Day Milestones**: 1,000 Days, 5,000 Days, 10,000 Days (10k), 15k, 20k, 25k, and 30,000 Days on Earth.
- **Second Milestones**: 100M, 500M, **1 Billion Seconds (The Gigasecond)**, 1.5B, 2B, and 3 Billion Seconds!
- Unlocked vs. Upcoming progress bar, filter toggles, and celebratory confetti triggers.

### 4. 🚀 Cosmic Journey & Planetary Ages
- **Biological Cycles**: Cumulative heartbeats (~80 bpm), breaths (~16 bpm), eye blinks while awake, meals eaten, and total sleep years.
- **Space Voyage**: Distance traveled orbiting the Sun (~67,000 mph) and orbiting the Milky Way Galaxy (~514,000 mph) in both Miles and Kilometers.
- **Planetary Ages**: Exact age and next birthday dates on Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto with orbital periods and planetary facts.

### 5. 🔮 Astrology, Generations & Cultural Lore
- **Western Zodiac**: Sign, astrological dates, ruling planet, element, quality, personality traits, and lucky numbers.
- **Chinese Zodiac**: Animal year, element (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water), Yin/Yang energy, virtues, and complementary animals.
- **Generational Cohorts**: Greatest Generation, Silent Gen, Baby Boomers, Gen X, Millennials (Gen Y), Gen Z, and Gen Alpha with era taglines and iconic technologies.
- **Birthstone & Birth Flower**: Monthly gemstone and botanical lore.
- **Day-of-the-Week Poem**: Traditional nursery rhyme ("Monday's child is fair of face...").

### 6. 📅 Life in Weeks (Memento Mori Grid)
- Interactive 90-year life grid consisting of **4,680 individual weekly squares** (52 weeks × 90 years).
- Color-coded life stages: Early Childhood, School Years, Higher Education & Exploration, Career & Prime Productivity, Mastery & Leadership, and Wisdom & Retirement.
- Hover inspection to reveal exact age, calendar year, and week index.

### 7. ⚖️ Two-Person Age Comparison & Double Age Day
- Compare two birth dates side-by-side.
- Computes exact chronological age difference and who is older.
- **Double Age Day**: Calculates the unique mathematical calendar date when the older person was or will be exactly 2.000× the age of the younger person.

### 8. 📸 Share & Export Card
- Beautiful shareable summary card.
- Download as PNG image (`html-to-image`) or copy formatted report to clipboard.
- Built-in multi-colored confetti celebrations (`canvas-confetti`).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework**: [Next.js 14](https://nextjs.org/) (App Router)
- **Language**: [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/)
- **Styling**: [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/)
- **Icons**: [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
- **Visuals & Effects**: `canvas-confetti`, `html-to-image`

## 🚀 Running the Project

In the project root directory:

```bash
# Start the local development server
npm run dev

# Or start the production build
npm start
```

Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.
